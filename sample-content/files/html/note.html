dooc / Release notes Sample release · 0.1

A clearer view of your work.

Keep the explanation, the source, and the conversation together. A small release with more room for the details that matter.

Three things to try

  1. 01 / SOURCE

    Files in context

    Bring code, diagrams, and data into a document. Keep a direct path back to the original file.

  2. 02 / FOCUS

    A view for every pane

    Compare light and dark previews side by side, with a theme for each pane.

  3. 03 / CONTEXT

    Details on demand

    Fold service cards into a compact summary. Open the details when you need the full story.
