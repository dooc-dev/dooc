The sidebar chooses what to open and which workspace tools to use. The document tree follows `document.children` in `dooc.yaml`; its selected page appears in the active preview pane.

Select the site name at the top to open the root document. The name is highlighted while the root is open; its children appear in the tree below. In edit mode, focus the name and press F2 to rename it, or double-click it.

Titles that link to another document, site page or website are underlined and use the link color. Ordinary content titles keep their normal styling, and no auxiliary destination icon is shown. Regular clicks open content inside dooc; middle clicks and Ctrl/Cmd-clicks open another tab. Web URLs open directly in that tab. On phones, or after a detected iframe failure, dooc asks before opening a Web URL externally. See [Clicks and link titles](contents/md/manifest.md#clicks-and-link-titles).
